# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are prospective, new, and current dental students in Malta, alongside external partners and dental professionals. Students seek information about MADS, its events, opportunities, student support, and ways to take part; partners and professionals need a clear way to understand MADS and connect with the association. Equal audience priority was confirmed by the user.

## Product Purpose

MADS represents, connects, and supports dental students throughout university and beyond. It provides a collective student voice and creates opportunities for academic, professional, and personal development. Success means students can find current, trustworthy information, discover ways to participate, and reach the appropriate MADS or official contact.

## Positioning

MADS is the student association connecting Malta's dental disciplines: Dental Surgery, Dental Hygiene, Dental Technology, and Dental Assistance. Its distinct role is to bring these students together while representing their shared and discipline-specific perspectives.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the public site to learn about MADS, find events and opportunities, discover outreach and student news, identify the right contact, and subscribe to approved updates. The site links to MADS's public Google Calendar. Public listings must remain separate from private committee material.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing routes cover Home, Events, Opportunities, Outreach, News & Photos, About MADS, Contact, and Privacy. Keep these routes, their core information, and working behaviors during the visual redesign.
- The site's event calendar and opportunity, news, representative, and link areas contain sample or pending-confirmation content. Preserve clear labels; do not imply that unverified items are real or current.
- Contact and newsletter forms show a configuration boundary and privacy-review note. Do not claim successful delivery or remove consent and privacy information without verified configuration and approval.
- Event registration, when present, opens the named external provider. This website does not process event payments.
- MADS does not replace official Faculty, University, medical, wellbeing, or emergency services.
- Use existing MADS-approved committee and event photographs. Respect photo consent, download-expiry, and privacy policies.
- The visual redesign must remain responsive across phone, tablet, laptop, and desktop layouts and preserve usable navigation, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Malta Association of Dental Students (MADS).
- Keep the MADS logo recognizable; the user previously authorized colour adaptations but asked that its design not be substantially changed.
- The user has supplied MADS-approved purpose copy and existing public-facing copy. Do not invent institutional claims, live events, verified opportunities, partner endorsements, or service commitments.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-provided MADS purpose and mission text in this conversation.
- Existing site pages, data, event placeholders, opportunity placeholders, contact guide, newsletter and contact forms.
- MADS committee and event photographs in the project assets and user-provided folders.
- A user-provided shareable Google Calendar URL in the site's calendar data.
- No verified live event feed, opportunity source feed, newsletter delivery status, or contact-form delivery commitment is established by the current source content.

## Product Principles

- Help students find events and ways to participate.
- Represent the four dental programmes without implying that pending representation details are confirmed.
- Make public information traceable to an approved source and label pending details honestly.
- Direct students to appropriate official services when MADS is not the responsible service.
- Keep the site's existing scope and useful page coverage; do not add new product features as part of a visual redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must work across phone, tablet, laptop, and desktop screen sizes. Preserve readable text, keyboard-operable navigation and controls, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
